Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Word Search Worlds ("we", "our", or "the app") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding information collection, usage, and protection when you use our mobile game.

Summary: We do not collect or store any personal identifying data. Your game progress is stored strictly on your local device, and ads are served through Google AdMob.

1. Information Collection & Storage

Word Search Worlds is designed purely for fun and cognitive entertainment:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require account registration or login. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, address, or contact lists.
  • Local Device Storage: Game statistics, completed levels, high scores, theme preferences, and coin/hint balances are stored strictly on your device locally and are never uploaded to private servers.

2. App Permissions & Usage

The app only requests standard permissions strictly necessary for its functionality:

  • Network Permissions (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively to deliver ads via Google AdMob and verify network connectivity status.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used solely to provide optional haptic touch feedback when finding words or completing levels.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Word Search Worlds free for everyone, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob and its advertising technology partners may automatically collect and process certain non-personal device information, including:

  • Anonymized device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / AAID).
  • App interaction and ad performance metrics.
  • Coarse regional location (derived from IP address).

All ad data processing is carried out in strict compliance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more by visiting Google Advertising Policies.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is built to provide family-friendly puzzle gameplay. If you believe any personal data from a child has inadvertently reached third-party ad services, please contact us immediately.

5. Data Security

Because Word Search Worlds does not operate external databases storing user identities or sensitive data, there is no risk of your personal information being compromised from our servers.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. The revised policy will always be posted directly on this page with the updated revision date.
